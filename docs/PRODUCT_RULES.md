# Product Rules

1. All times are stored safely and displayed in the configured office timezone.
2. Attendance date is based on the office timezone, not the browser timezone.
3. One employee can have at most one attendance row per calendar day.
4. Late is determined by configured office start time plus grace period.
5. Approved leave must be distinguishable from absence.
6. Pending leave/late requests are not treated as approved.
7. Employees cannot silently modify historical attendance.
8. Admin corrections require an audit log.
9. Team views expose only information permitted by company policy.
10. Private leave reasons and attachments should not be exposed to coworkers by default.
11. Deactivated users retain historical records but cannot authenticate.
12. Deleting employees should generally be avoided; use deactivation to preserve history.
